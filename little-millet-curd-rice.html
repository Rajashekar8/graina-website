Little Millet Curd Rice

A simple, wholesome Graina recipe using grains from our range. Cooking time and water needs can vary with the grain variety and brand.

Ingredients

  • Little millet
  • curd
  • milk or water
  • mustard seeds
  • urad dal
  • ginger
  • curry leaves

How to prepare

Cook little millet until soft and let it cool until lukewarm. Mix with curd and a little milk or water. Temper mustard seeds, urad dal, ginger and curry leaves in oil and mix into the millet. Adjust salt and serve.
