Foxtail Millet Upma

A simple, wholesome Graina recipe using grains from our range. Cooking time and water needs can vary with the grain variety and brand.

Ingredients

  • Foxtail millet
  • onion
  • carrot
  • peas
  • green chilli
  • mustard seeds
  • curry leaves

How to prepare

Rinse and soak the millet for 15 minutes, then drain. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in a little oil. Add onion, chilli and vegetables; sauté. Add millet, salt and the water recommended for your millet. Cover and cook on low until tender. Rest for 5 minutes and fluff.
