Foxtail Millet Khichdi

A warm, comforting one-pot meal made with Korra Biyyam, yellow moong dal, colourful vegetables and gentle Indian spices. Simple to prepare and lovely for lunch or dinner.

About 60 minutes including soaking 2 servings Vegetarian Easy

A nourishing bowl of millet goodness

This Graina-style khichdi brings together foxtail millet and moong dal with vegetables for a soft, satisfying texture. Adjust the water to make it fluffy or pleasantly porridge-like.

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 1/2 cup hulled foxtail millet (Korra Biyyam / Korralu)
  • 1/2 cup yellow split moong dal
  • 2 1/2 to 3 cups water, as needed
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables: carrot, beans, peas and capsicum
  • 1 small tomato, chopped (optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped spinach or methi (optional)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp ghee or cooking oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 green chilli, chopped, or chilli powder to taste
  • 1/8 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon to finish

How to make millet khichdi

  1. Wash and soak. Rinse the foxtail millet and moong dal separately or together until the water runs mostly clear. Soak for about 30 minutes, then drain well.
  2. Start the tempering. Heat ghee or oil in a pressure cooker. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle. Add grated ginger and green chilli; stir briefly until fragrant.
  3. Sauté the vegetables. Add carrot, beans, peas, capsicum and tomato. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring so the vegetables are coated with the seasoning.
  4. Add spices, millet and dal. Stir in turmeric, chilli and salt. Add the drained millet and moong dal, and sauté gently for another minute.
  5. Pour in water and cook. Add 2 1/2 cups water and mix. Close the pressure cooker and cook on medium heat for about 2 to 3 whistles. Turn off the heat and allow the pressure to release naturally.
  6. Finish and serve. Open the cooker and stir gently. Add a little hot water if you prefer a softer khichdi. Mix in chopped coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Serve hot.
Texture tip: For a soft, porridge-style khichdi, add a little more hot water after cooking. For a thicker, grainier texture, begin with the lower water quantity and adjust only if needed.

Open-pot method

Follow the same washing, soaking and sautéing steps in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add about 3 cups of water, cover and simmer on low heat, stirring occasionally, until the millet and dal are soft. Add a splash of hot water if the pot becomes too dry before the grains are cooked. Cooking time varies with the millet and the age of the grain, so check for tenderness before serving.

Graina kitchen tips

  • Choose hulled, food-grade foxtail millet for this recipe.
  • Soaking helps the grains cook more evenly and can shorten cooking time.
  • Chop vegetables into small, similar-sized pieces.
  • For a milder flavour, skip the chilli and use ginger and cumin.
  • For a richer finish, add a small drizzle of ghee just before serving.

Serve it with

  • Plain curd or raita
  • Lemon or mango pickle
  • Roasted papad
  • Freshly chopped coriander
