Little Millet Vegetable Pulao

A simple, wholesome Graina recipe using grains from our range. Cooking time and water needs can vary with the grain variety and brand.

Ingredients

  • Little millet
  • mixed vegetables
  • onion
  • whole spices
  • ginger-garlic
  • mint

How to prepare

Rinse and soak millet briefly if desired. Sauté whole spices, onion and ginger-garlic. Add vegetables, mint, salt and pulao spices. Stir in drained millet and the appropriate quantity of water. Cover and cook until tender; rest before fluffing.
