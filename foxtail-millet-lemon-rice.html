Foxtail Millet Lemon Rice

A delicious, home-style foxtail millet lemon rice recipe made with wholesome Graina grains. Follow the simple steps below for a satisfying everyday meal.

25 minutes 2 servings Vegetarian Easy

Goodness in every grain

Cook with the grains you love from Graina. This recipe includes a clear ingredient list, step-by-step method and practical kitchen tips. Adjust seasoning to your taste.

Ingredients

For 2 servings

  • 1 cup foxtail millet
  • 2 to 2 1/4 cups water, as needed
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 1 green chilli, slit
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice, or to taste
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander

How to make Foxtail Millet Lemon Rice

  1. Rinse the foxtail millet thoroughly. Cook it with water and a little salt until tender but separate. Spread on a plate and let it cool slightly.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds; when they splutter, add urad dal and peanuts. Cook until the dal and peanuts turn lightly golden.
  3. Add curry leaves, green chilli and turmeric. Stir briefly, then turn off the heat.
  4. Add lemon juice and salt to the tempering. Mix in the cooked millet gently so the grains stay separate.
  5. Taste and adjust lemon and salt. Garnish with coriander and serve.
Graina kitchen tip: Let the cooked millet cool before mixing in the lemon tempering. This helps keep the grains separate and prevents them from becoming mushy.

Serving suggestions

Enjoy your freshly prepared foxtail millet lemon rice with:

  • Cucumber raita
  • Vegetable salad
  • Papad
