Oats Porridge

A simple, wholesome Graina recipe using grains from our range. Cooking time and water needs can vary with the grain variety and brand.

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats
  • milk or water
  • banana or seasonal fruit
  • nuts
  • cinnamon

How to prepare

Add oats and milk or water to a saucepan. Simmer, stirring, until creamy and the oats are soft. Add cinnamon and top with sliced fruit and nuts. Adjust thickness with extra milk or water.
